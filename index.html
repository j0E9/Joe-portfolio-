Buried-person detection radar

01 / SIMULATE

Model the ground before trusting it.

A physics-based ground-penetrating radar simulator: propagation, attenuation, link budget, depth and SNR, A-scans and radargrams through wet soil, saturated clay, mud, rock and mixed layers.

02 / GROUND IT

Real soil, real places.

SoilGrids data builds location-specific soil profiles for Munnar and Wayanad, the Kerala hills where landslides actually happen.

03 / DETECT

Teach a model to hear a weak echo.

Synthetic radar datasets and ML experiments for weak-echo detection, with failure analysis across frequency, conductivity and depth. Now working on stationary targets under static clutter using multi-scan analysis and spatial subtraction.
